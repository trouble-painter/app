Trouble Painter is an online drawing game, a drawing liar and mafia game for friends. Everyone draws one picture together, one stroke at a time, but one player is secretly the Trouble Painter (Hamster) who has no idea what the Good Painters (Bear) are drawing. Watch every line, guess who is faking it, and unmask the liar before they ruin the artwork!

Whether you love party games, drawing games, liar games or mafia games, Trouble Painter turns a few friends and a phone into a fast, funny and noisy game night. No drawing skills needed: the worse the sketch, the bigger the laughs.

What is Trouble Painter?
A real-time multiplayer drawing mafia game for 3 to 10 players. The Good Painters are given a secret keyword and take turns adding a single stroke to a shared canvas. The Trouble Painter only knows the category, so they must bluff, blend in and draw just like everyone else. Can you tell who is the imposter?

How to Play
1. Gather 3 to 10 players, create a room and share the invitation code, QR code or link.
2. Once everyone is ready, each player is randomly assigned a role and keyword.
   Trouble Painter: Only knows the category. Draw without being discovered and try to figure out the keyword.
   Good Painter: Knows the keyword. Draw it, but don't make it so obvious that the Trouble Painter can guess it.
3. The game has 2 rounds, and every player can draw only one stroke per turn.
4. After everyone has drawn, vote in real time on who you think the Trouble Painter is within the time limit.
5. If the Trouble Painter gets the most votes, they get one chance to guess the keyword.
6. If the Trouble Painter guesses the keyword, or is not caught, they win. Otherwise the Good Painters win!

FAQ
Q. How many players do I need?
A. You need at least 3 players, and up to 10 players can join one room.

Q. Do I need to be good at drawing?
A. Not at all. Clever, funny and suspicious drawings are what make this drawing game exciting.

Q. How do I invite my friends?
A. Create a room and share the invitation code, show the QR code, or send the link.

Q. What if I don't have friends to play with right now?
A. Tap Quick Start to join a match with other players.

Q. What is the difference from other mafia and liar games?
A. Instead of just talking, you play by drawing. Your strokes are your clues, and every line can give you away.
